UAE Culture: Respectful Visiting Tips and Local Customs

The UAE is home to people from many backgrounds, alongside deep Emirati traditions and Islamic cultural influences. Visitors and residents can show respect by being considerate of local customs and the people around them.

UAE Culture & Lifestyle

Dress with awareness

Dress requirements vary by venue. In malls, government buildings, places of worship and traditional settings, modest clothing is a respectful choice. Mosques may have specific dress rules for visitors; check the venue guidance before arriving.

Greetings and hospitality

A polite greeting and patience are appreciated. Some people prefer not to shake hands with someone of another gender for religious or personal reasons; follow the other person's lead rather than assuming.

Photography and privacy

Ask permission before photographing people, especially families, children or workers. Do not photograph security-sensitive locations or private property where photography is restricted.

Ramadan and religious occasions

During Ramadan, be mindful of fasting colleagues and neighbours and follow current local rules and venue guidance. Opening hours and public arrangements may change during religious periods.

Public behaviour and online conduct

Be considerate in public spaces and avoid confrontational behaviour, offensive gestures or insulting comments. UAE laws apply to online communication as well as conduct in person.

Learn from local sources

Customs and venue requirements can vary. Follow official UAE government and destination guidance, and approach differences with curiosity and respect.
