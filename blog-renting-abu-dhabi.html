What Should I Know Before Renting in Abu Dhabi?

Before renting in Abu Dhabi, compare the full cost of the tenancy and make sure the contract, registration, payment terms and property condition are clear. A careful check before signing can prevent surprises later.

Renting

Check the total annual cost

Look beyond the advertised rent. Ask about the number and timing of payments, security deposit, agency fee, municipality or other applicable charges, utilities, cooling, parking and any community fees payable by the tenant.

Review the tenancy contract

Check the tenant and landlord names, property details, rent, payment dates, term, renewal provisions, maintenance responsibilities, early termination clauses and notice requirements. Do not sign a blank or incomplete contract.

Confirm Tawtheeq registration

Ask how the tenancy will be registered through the applicable Abu Dhabi process and what documents are required. Verify the current requirements with the relevant authority or service channel.

Inspect before moving in

Record the condition of the property with dated photographs and a signed inventory where available. Test air conditioning, water, appliances, locks, windows and access cards, and report defects in writing.

Understand maintenance responsibilities

Clarify who is responsible for routine and major repairs, emergency call-outs, air-conditioning servicing and damage caused by misuse. Ensure the contract wording is understood.

Keep a complete record

Retain the signed contract, registration confirmation, receipts, inspection report and all written communications. For a dispute, check the current Abu Dhabi tenancy rules and seek qualified advice.

Important: This article is general educational information, not legal, mortgage, tax or investment advice. Laws, fees and procedures can change and may differ between emirates, projects and individual contracts. Verify current requirements with the relevant authority and obtain independent professional advice before making a decision.
