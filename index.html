Property Advice · Buying a Home

Why Is Property Snagging SO Important?

Buying a property is a major investment. Whether you are purchasing a brand-new home, an off-plan property at handover, or a resale property, it is important to understand exactly what you are receiving.

Property snagging is a detailed inspection designed to identify visible defects, incomplete works and areas that may require attention before you accept the property or move in.

What does a snagging inspection look for?

  • Cracks, paintwork, plastering and wall finishes.
  • Doors, windows, locks, handles and alignment.
  • Flooring, tiles, grouting and skirting.
  • Kitchen cupboards, worktops and fitted appliances.
  • Bathrooms, sanitaryware, taps and visible plumbing issues.
  • Electrical fittings, switches, sockets and lighting.
  • Air conditioning operation and visible ventilation concerns.
  • External areas, balconies, terraces and visible finishing defects.

Why should you arrange snagging before handover?

A professional inspection can help you document defects and incomplete items clearly, giving you a structured list to raise with the developer or relevant contractor. It can also help reduce the risk of overlooking issues when you are excited to receive your keys and move into your new home.

Is snagging only for new-build properties?

No. A resale property can also benefit from a thorough inspection. Understanding the visible condition of a property before completion can help you make better-informed decisions and identify matters that may need further investigation.

Ship My Furniture or Buy New After Relocation?

Moving to the UAE is an exciting new chapter, but one question often comes up before the move: should you bring your existing furniture with you, or start again and buy new once you arrive?

There is no single answer. The best decision depends on the size and quality of your furniture, shipping costs, your accommodation, how long you intend to stay and what matters most to your family.

When shipping your furniture may make sense

  • You have quality furniture that would be expensive to replace.
  • You have sentimental or personal items you want to keep.
  • You are relocating long-term or permanently.
  • Your new property has a suitable layout and enough space.
  • You have compared the full shipping and customs costs.

When buying new in the UAE may be better

  • You are moving into a furnished or partially furnished home.
  • Your relocation is temporary or you are unsure how long you will stay.
  • Your existing furniture is costly to ship relative to its replacement value.
  • You would prefer furniture that fits your new property and lifestyle.
  • You want to avoid the time and administration involved in international shipping.

Remember to calculate the full cost

Shipping is not just the price of transporting a container. Ask for a complete quotation that explains packing, collection, transport, insurance, customs procedures, delivery and any additional charges. For buying new, consider delivery, assembly, warranties and the cost of furnishing the entire property.

Our advice

Before making a decision, obtain your shipping quotation and compare it with a realistic replacement budget in the UAE. It can also help to view your new property or confirm its floor plan before deciding what to bring.
