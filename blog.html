Why Is Property Snagging SO Important?

Buying a property is one of the biggest financial commitments you may ever make. Whether you have purchased a brand-new apartment, townhouse or villa in Abu Dhabi, receiving the keys does not necessarily mean everything is finished to the standard you expected.

This is where property snagging becomes so important. A snagging inspection is a detailed assessment of a property to identify visible defects, incomplete works, damage and areas that may require correction before you accept handover.

1. Identify defects before accepting handover

Small issues can be easy to overlook during an excited first viewing. A professional inspection takes a more methodical approach, checking the property room by room and recording observations clearly.

Items that may be checked include:

  • Paintwork, plastering and wall finishes.
  • Flooring, tiles, grout and skirting boards.
  • Doors, windows, locks and alignment.
  • Kitchen cabinets, worktops and fittings.
  • Bathrooms, sanitaryware and visible plumbing issues.
  • Electrical sockets, switches and lighting.
  • Air-conditioning operation and visible ventilation concerns.
  • Balconies, terraces and external finishes where accessible.

2. Protect your investment

Your new property should reflect the quality and specification you purchased. Identifying problems early gives you an opportunity to document them and raise them with the developer or responsible party through the appropriate handover process.

3. Make sure issues are properly documented

A useful snagging report does more than list general complaints. It should identify the location of each observation, explain what was found and include supporting photographs wherever appropriate. A clear report makes it easier to communicate outstanding items and follow up on rectification.

4. Avoid unnecessary disruption after moving in

Discovering defects after furniture has been delivered and you have settled into your home can be frustrating. Early inspections can help identify visible issues before the property is occupied and furnished.

5. A thorough inspection matters

A quick walk-through is not the same as a detailed snagging inspection. Depending on the property, a comprehensive report may cover hundreds of individual observations, with photographs and clear descriptions.

Royal Bridges tip: Arrange your inspection as early as the handover process allows. Keep copies of the report, photographs, correspondence and any responses received regarding outstanding items.

Ship My Furniture or Buy New After Relocation?

Relocating to Abu Dhabi or elsewhere in the UAE is an exciting step, but one of the first practical questions many people face is what to do with their existing furniture.

Should you ship everything from home, bring only your favourite pieces, or start again once you arrive? There is no single answer. The right decision depends on your furniture, your new property, your budget and how long you intend to stay.

1. Start with your new property's layout

Before arranging shipping, find out the size and layout of your new home. A large sofa or dining table that fitted perfectly in your previous property may not suit the room dimensions of your UAE accommodation.

Compare measurements, doorways, lift dimensions and available storage before deciding what to bring.

2. Compare the full shipping cost

International furniture shipping involves more than the initial transport quotation. Depending on the route and service, you may need to consider packing, collection, freight, insurance, customs documentation, destination handling and delivery.

Ask for a written quotation that explains what is included and what could cost extra. Check current UAE import requirements before shipping personal belongings.

3. Consider what is worth bringing

Furniture with sentimental value, good quality solid wood pieces or items that are difficult to replace may be worth shipping. Older, bulky or inexpensive furniture may be more practical to sell, donate or leave behind.

4. Buying new in the UAE

The UAE has a wide range of furniture retailers, from affordable everyday options to bespoke and luxury suppliers. Buying locally can make it easier to choose pieces that suit your new property's dimensions and style.

Remember to account for delivery times, assembly, installation and the cost of furnishing the entire home rather than just individual items.

5. A combination may be the best option

Many relocating families choose to bring selected personal belongings and favourite furniture while buying larger or less practical items after arrival.

Our advice: Do not book an international shipment until you know where you will be living, what will fit and what the complete shipping cost will be. A little planning can make your move considerably easier.
