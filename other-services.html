Property Inspection

Property Snagging

A property may look perfect during a viewing, but defects can be easier to identify through a systematic inspection. Snagging helps buyers and owners document visible issues and outstanding finishing works before accepting a property or completing a handover.

Pre-Handover Inspections

Inspection enquiries before accepting a newly built or recently completed property.

Detailed Snag Reports

Organized reporting of observed defects, incomplete works and finishing concerns.

Photographic Evidence

Photographs to help identify and communicate the location of reported issues.

Interior Checks

Visual checks of doors, windows, flooring, walls, ceilings, fixtures and finishes.

Services & Fixtures

Visual observations of accessible fittings and fixtures, subject to inspection scope.

Follow-Up Inspections

Enquiries about re-inspection after reported items have been addressed.

A snagging inspection is a visual inspection within an agreed scope. It is not a substitute for specialist structural, engineering, electrical or mechanical testing where those services are required.
