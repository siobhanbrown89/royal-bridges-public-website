External Agencies & Freelance Agents

A Collaborative Network Built Around Opportunity

Royal Bridges welcomes licensed freelance property professionals and established agencies who want to expand their reach, share suitable opportunities and work together to close transactions. Whether you have your own client and property, need access to our stock and leads, or want to connect with other agents through our portal, there is a collaboration route to suit the way you work.

↗

Bring Your Own Lead & Unit

Source your own client and property opportunity and manage the transaction in collaboration with Royal Bridges. The agreed agent share is 80% of the applicable commission, with 20% retained by Royal Bridges.

◎

Work With Our Stock & Leads

Access suitable Royal Bridges property stock or client leads where available. Where the opportunity is provided by Royal Bridges, the commission split is 60% to the freelance agent and 40% to Royal Bridges.

⇄

Connect With Other Agents

List your available units on the Royal Bridges portal and connect with other agents who may have the right buyer, tenant or investor. Work together directly through Royal Bridges using the three-way sharing model below.

✦

Company Referral & Loyalty Programme

Companies, including relocation businesses, can recommend Royal Bridges to their clients as part of our Loyalty Programme. Each successful referral has a financial value. For each referred client who completes a transaction through Royal Bridges, the referring company may elect one of two benefits: a 10% share of the commission received by Royal Bridges, or a 10% discount against the commission charged on the referred client’s transaction. The chosen benefit must be confirmed when the referral is registered and documented in writing, subject to applicable UAE requirements.

Freelance Agent Commission

Clear Splits. Different Ways to Work.

We recognise that agents contribute in different ways. These commission structures distinguish between opportunities you source independently and opportunities supplied through Royal Bridges.

Option 1 — Your Lead & Your Unit

For a transaction where you introduce your own client and bring the property unit or off-plan sale opportunity.

80%Freelance agent
20%Royal Bridges

Higher share to you: the freelance agent receives 80% and Royal Bridges retains 20% of the applicable commission.

Option 2 — Royal Bridges Stock or Leads

For a transaction where you work with a property from our stock or a client lead introduced by Royal Bridges.

60%Freelance agent
40%Royal Bridges

Higher share to you: the freelance agent receives 60% and Royal Bridges retains 40% of the applicable commission.

Commission percentages refer to the applicable commission actually received for a completed transaction, unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. The transaction, source of the lead/unit, responsibilities, payment timing and any applicable deductions should be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Royal Bridges Agent Portal

List Your Units. Find the Right Agent. Close Together.

Agents can submit their available rental, resale and off-plan units for listing on the Royal Bridges portal. The network gives participating agents the opportunity to discover one another's stock and connect directly where they have a matching client or requirement.

When one agent lists the unit and another agent introduces the successful client, the commission is shared using the following structure.

Agent-to-Agent Collaboration Split

45%Listing agent
45%Closing agent
10%Royal Bridges

The 45 / 45 / 10 model applies to rental, resale and off-plan sales completed through this portal collaboration route: 45% to the agent supplying the unit, 45% to the agent closing with the client, and 10% to Royal Bridges.
