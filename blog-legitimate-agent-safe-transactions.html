How Do I Ensure My Agent Is Legitimate and Transactions Are Safe?

A professional property transaction should be transparent, documented and independently verifiable. A few checks before sharing documents or transferring money can help reduce avoidable risks.

Buying Property

Verify the agent and brokerage

Ask for the agent's full name, brokerage details and current licence or registration credentials. Check them through the relevant official regulator or licensing authority for the emirate where the property is located.

Confirm the property details

For a ready property, verify ownership and authority to market or sell. For off-plan, check the project and developer registration with the relevant authority and ensure the details match the SPA and payment instructions.

Keep agreements in writing

Record the agreed price, commission, deposit, inclusions, deadlines and any conditions. Read the agency agreement and transaction forms before signing, and ask for an explanation of anything unclear.

Protect your money and identity

Only use verified payment instructions and the correct beneficiary. Be cautious of last-minute bank-detail changes, personal-account requests, pressure to pay immediately or requests to share one-time passwords.

Check every document

Review the MOU or sale agreement, SPA, title details, NOC, mortgage documents and receipts. Make sure names, unit numbers, amounts and dates are consistent.

If something feels wrong

Pause the transaction and verify independently with the regulator, bank or qualified UAE lawyer. Do not rely on a contact number or link supplied in a suspicious payment request.

Important: This article is general educational information, not legal, mortgage, tax or investment advice. Laws, fees and procedures can change and may differ between emirates, projects and individual contracts. Verify current requirements with the relevant authority and obtain independent professional advice before making a decision.
